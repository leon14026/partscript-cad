occt-wasm 5.17.2 / Open CASCADE Technology
The occt-wasm wrapper is available under MIT or Apache-2.0. The compiled
Open CASCADE Technology module is distributed under LGPL-2.1 with the
Open CASCADE exception. The source and build scripts are available at:
https://github.com/andymai/occt-wasm/tree/1c8cb49956fd3bf9717a7688deb79a3fc37e01e8
Use a recursive checkout for the pinned OCCT source submodule.
https://dev.opencascade.org/resources/licensing
The runtime is loaded from separate .js and .wasm assets. To substitute a
compatible rebuild of the engine, replace the module and binary in the build
or update their URLs in src/engine/geometry.worker.ts and rebuild the app.
